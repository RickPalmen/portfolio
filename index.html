About

Minimal, but purposeful. I bring ideas to life through bold, deliberate design that looks sharp, feels right, and leaves a lasting impression — every project is a small chapter in a bigger story I'm still writing.

23 years old, drawn to clean, fitting aesthetics and bold type — calm by nature, curious by habit. I find energy in big cities, matching colour palettes, and the details most people overlook.

Introverted by nature, I like to take my time to observe, think, and create — all while staying chronically online, saving inspiration and chasing whatever's next. My dad always told me to do what I love and work hard for it, and I carry that with me into every project.
